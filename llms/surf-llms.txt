# Surf AI

> Surf AI (asksurf.ai) is a crypto-focused AI research and execution platform offering professional chat with Instant and Thinking modes, large-scale crypto news aggregation, Cryptopedia project intelligence with pre-TGE reports, on-chain analytics across 40+ blockchains, and AI-powered research report generation.

Surf provides crypto intelligence through AI chat, crypto news aggregation (News/Feed), and Cryptopedia covering 1000+ crypto projects with real-time price data, social sentiment from 100k+ KOLs, 200+ technical indicators, and deep research capabilities scanning 200+ websites.
